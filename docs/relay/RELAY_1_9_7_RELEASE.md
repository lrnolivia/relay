# Relay 1.9.7 — creative / platform / performance skill packs

Relay 1.9.7 turns the 1.9.5 skills-runtime foundation and 1.9.6.2 feature-team organization into a coherent creative operating layer.

## Ships

- Inspector-first art direction and anti-slop judgment;
- information architecture and flow guidance;
- motion/microinteraction guidance with truthful liveness and reduced-motion rules;
- visual QA/design-editing guidance;
- platform craft packs for web, macOS/SwiftUI, Windows/WinUI, Android/Material, and GNOME/GTK/libadwaita;
- Figma orchestration that requires the real capability and degrades to an implementation-neutral spec when absent;
- performance-budget and visual-performance guidance that protects ambitious art direction instead of flattening it by default;
- a deterministic team-aware composition contract: assignment -> team -> role -> sticky staff -> smallest useful skill combination.

## Organization

`inspector` is the primary creative authority. Valentina leads art direction, Vivienne flow/product structure, Margot design editing, Sabine motion, and Roman verification. `runner` owns implementation/execution; `night-shift` owns planning/continuity; source/cloud/release/skills remain specialist teams. Cross-team participation does not rewrite home-team identity and never changes authorization.

## Design law

Skill packs are composable, not a permanent mega-prompt. Load only what the assignment needs.

The creative layer rejects generic SaaS slop, arbitrary gradients, fake depth, equal-weight card grids, and effects without a purpose. It favors a coherent visual thesis, platform-native interaction, semantic information hierarchy, and measured performance.

## Relay card direction carried forward

Conversational cards use the same feature-panel language as the web product: transparent host canvas; large lowercase feature identity; matching semantic icon and bounded pulse/glow; short human blurb; a rounded information panel with the most pertinent large metric/data; actions underneath; and a compact primary-reporter badge when staff identity is useful. Rendering proof is not design acceptance.

## Verification

1.9.7 is a skill-pack release. Its guidance must be discoverable from canonical project/assignment metadata without granting new authority. Figma-specific behavior must never claim work when Figma is unavailable. Performance guidance must accompany ambitious visuals rather than silently deleting them.
